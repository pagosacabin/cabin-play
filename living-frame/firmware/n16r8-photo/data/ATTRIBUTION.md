# Source photographs

## hummingbird.jpg (used from 0228 onward)

- Title: *Antillean crested hummingbird feeding*
- Author: **Charles J. Sharp**
- Licence: **CC BY 3.0**
- Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Antillean_crested_hummingbird_feeding.jpg
- Retrieved: 2026-11-20 from Wikimedia Commons (1846x1343 original)
- Used here as the living frame's test photograph, at Stephen's request: the
  earlier test image (`photo-display-s3/data/parrot.jpg`, two lorikeets on a
  feeder) judged badly on the panel — busy composition and large dark areas, which
  is the worst case for 64 colours.

Chosen for the opposite properties: a bright subject, warm bokeh rather than
large black fields, and smooth out-of-focus gradients, so the dither has
something to blend rather than something to starve.

`photo-display-s3/data/parrot.jpg` and `data/photo.jpg` (blue marble) remain the
0179/0180-era test images and are untouched.
